Experience that shows

30+ years of pattern precision.

Accuracy is what matters most. Modern software is only a tool—the true fit quality comes from understanding how flat fabric wraps around a human body. By applying master hand-drafting principles directly to digital pattern creation, we eliminate fit and proportion errors common in purely computer-generated patterns.

May has worked as a high-end pattern maker for more than 30 years. After immigrating from Korea, she built her craft in Los Angeles, developing a reputation for meticulous accuracy. Today, May Pattern Studio offers brands the convenience of clean, digitized files backed by decades of authentic artisan mastery.

What we offer

Services

Professional pattern support for apparel — from initial digital creation to graded production markers.

Digitized Pattern Making

Digital pattern creation backed by traditional hand-pattern expertise. We leverage decades of hands-on drafting precision to deliver production-ready digital pattern files with unmatched fit, balance, and quality.

Pattern Grading & Marking

Accurate size expansion and optimized production markers. We grade across your full size range while preserving original style proportions, maximizing fabric yield, and streamlining factory cutting.

Fit & Sample Adjustments

Pattern corrections based on fit sessions or sample feedback. Small refinements that prevent larger problems later in production.
